Hackathon 2026: 48 hours to build Smarters Cities

Published on October 12,2026 by the AI Club team

The Ai Club invites all student to its annual hackathon. In teams of three to five, you will design a prototype that makes our cities cleaner, safer, or easier to live in. No prior experience is required: mentors will help you all weekend.

Prizes

  1. First place: a two-week internship at a partner company
  2. Second place: development boards for each team member
  3. Third place: online course vouchers

Who can participate?

  • All ENSIA students, from first to fifth year
  • Teams must include:
    • at least one first- or second-year student
    • at most five members

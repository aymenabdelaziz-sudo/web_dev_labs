Hackathon 2026: 48 Hours to Build Smarter Cities

Published on October 12, 2026 by the AI Club team
  (machine-readable date: 2026-10-12)

Last year, 24 teams competed in the main hall.

The AI Club invites all students to its annual hackathon. In teams of three
to five, you will design a prototype that makes our cities cleaner, safer, or
easier to live in. No prior experience is required: mentors will help you all
weekend.
